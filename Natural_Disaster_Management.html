The Role Of Computing In Reducing The Risks Of Natural Disasters

Prediction Technology

Where a natural disaster is incredibly hard to prevent, such as in the case of a wildfire, instead of trying to stop the disaster from ever occuring, we can instead mitigate its risk by using predictive technology. this technology is used in order to plot the path that the fire is likely to travel, allowing for pre-emptive measures to be taken, stopping the fire from spreading beyond control. These kind of early measures are imperative, ensuring the best possible response to a disaster, and therefore minimizing the amount of damage caused.

Warning Systems

Alongside being able to plot information about disasters, computing is an extremely effective tool in providing information that may warn us of an impending natural disaster that is not immediately obvious. This is helpful especially in the case of earthquakes and tsunamis, giving people valuable time to get as far away from the epicentre/coastline, saving lives in the process. While this cannot particularly be used to reduce the damage caused by these disasters, the sheer value of saving human lives is a clear demonstration that computing is an extremely important industry.
