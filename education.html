How Technology Helps Us Learn

Technology is changing the way we study and making education easier for everyone.

Introduction

Technology is now an important part of education. Students use computers, phones and websites to learn new things every day.

Computing can help students with different needs, make lessons more interesting and allow people to study from almost anywhere.

Three Ways Technology Helps Education

1. Accessible Learning

Technology can make learning easier for students with different needs.

For example, students with hearing difficulties can use subtitles when watching online lessons. Screen readers can also help students with visual difficulties.

These tools allow more students to take part in education.

Examples:
  • Subtitles
  • Screen readers
  • Voice typing

2. Gamified Learning

Learning can sometimes be boring, especially when we need to remember a lot of information.

However, games and quizzes can make learning more interesting. One example is Kahoot, which allows students to answer questions and earn points.

I have played Kahoot in my previous classes. I think it is a fun way to learn because students can compete with each other and check what they know at the same time.

It also makes the classroom more interactive instead of just listening to the teacher.

Examples:
  • Kahoot
  • Duolingo
  • Online quizzes
Visit Kahoot

3. Learning Anywhere

In the past, students mainly learned from teachers and textbooks. Now, we can also learn through websites, videos and online courses.

For example, students can use YouTube to watch programming tutorials or Khan Academy to practise different subjects.

This is useful because students can study at their own speed and go back to lessons when they do not understand something.

Examples:
  • YouTube tutorials
  • Khan Academy
  • Online courses

Why Is Technology Important in Education?

Technology gives students more ways to learn. It can help people who have different learning needs, make lessons more enjoyable and give students access to learning resources outside the classroom.

Although technology cannot replace teachers, it can be a useful tool to support education.
