Health

Medicine produces more data than any person can review: scans, test results, genomes, prescriptions. Computing helps clinicians and researchers find the parts that matter, and helps different services see the same facts about the same patient.

Electronic health records

Electronic health records (EHRs) store and manage patient information digitally. They make information easier to reach, reduce paperwork and minimise errors. They also let patient data be shared between doctors, nurses and departments.

Diagnostic support

Diagnostic support systems assist doctors by analysing symptoms and suggesting possible conditions. Clinical decision-support tools are one example.

Medical imaging and analysis

Software is used to enhance and analyse medical images. 3D imaging helps surgeons plan operations, and AI can identify patterns and anomalies in scans.

Laboratory information systems

Laboratory information systems (LIS) manage test orders, samples and results. They automate workflows, reduce manual errors and speed up reporting.

Patient monitoring

Monitoring systems track vital signs continuously, for example in intensive care units, and raise alerts when readings are abnormal. They connect to the EHR so records update in real time.

Telemedicine

Telemedicine offers remote consultations by video call, which gives patients in remote areas access to care. It works through stored-and-forwarded data, real-time interaction and remote monitoring.

Hospital management

Hospital management systems handle administrative, financial and clinical operations. Their modules cover billing, pharmacy, inventory and scheduling.

Computer-aided surgery

Robotic surgery and image-guided procedures allow high precision in complex operations.

Research and data analysis

Statistical tools help analyse clinical trials and health data. Researchers also use them for epidemiological studies and for predictive models of disease outbreaks.

Pharmacy and drug management

E-prescriptions reduce errors and streamline repeat prescriptions. Drug-interaction checkers and inventory systems support pharmacists, and barcode-based medication administration helps make sure the right patient gets the right drug.

Training and simulation

Virtual reality and simulation systems let students practise surgery, emergency response and diagnosis without risk to real patients.

Wearables and health apps

Wearables and health apps track data such as heart rate. They can sync with clinical systems to support better management of long-term conditions.
