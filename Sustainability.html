Computing and Sustainability

Technology for sustainability

Although technology and sustainability are usually thought of as being polar opposite in nature, there are actually many ways that technology is being used to advance sustainability efforts and practices:

  1. HVAC Systems
    2. AI and Machine Learning
    Traditional HVAC (heating, ventilation, and cooling) systems rely on static methods to control conditions within buildings.
    With the integration of AI, HVAC systems can be adapted to respond dynamically to a multitude of conditions such as room occupancy levels and external weather conditions.
    As a result, energy waste within large buildings can be reduced by only powering appliances such as heaters and AC when required.
    This also means conditions within buildings can be set and adjusted more optimally for occupant comfort.
  2. Waste Management
    3. IoT (internet of things)
    Smart bins are fitted with sensors that can monitor bin capacity levels.
    This means that collection services can be alerted when waste collections are required, reducing unnecessary trips and providing optimised collection routes between the smart bin locations.
  3. Food Production
    4. Precision agriculture
    Drone and sensor technology is being used to provide farmers with real-time data on various farm operations such as pest control and irrigation levels.
    This means that farmers can make informed decisions on pesticide and water usage, reducing resource wastage and increasing production yields.
